# Vendor Management Policy

## Goals

- Track vendors that process customer data and ensure appropriate contracts and risk reviews.

## Minimum requirements

- Maintain a vendor inventory (name, purpose, data accessed, link to ToS/DPA).
- Classify vendor risk (low/medium/high) and document mitigations.
- Ensure least-privilege API access (scopes/permissions).
- Review vendors at least annually or upon material product changes.

## Common vendors in this repo (examples)

- Google (OAuth/Gmail APIs)
- Stripe (billing)
- OpenAI/LLM providers (model inference)
- BrightData / Weaviate (data collection/search)
